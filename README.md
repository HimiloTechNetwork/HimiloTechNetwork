# 👋 Welcome to HimiloTech Network

**HimiloTech Network** is a knowledge-driven initiative dedicated to empowering Somali youth across Europe and the Horn of Africa through IT education, digital literacy, and community-based learning.

We believe that access to technology and structured learning environments can transform lives. Our work is rooted in mentorship, open collaboration, and scalable systems that make technical knowledge accessible to all.

---

## 🎯 What We Do

- 🧠 **IT Education**: Project-based labs, tutorials, and bilingual resources (Somali & English)
- 🛠️ **Digital Empowerment**: Career guidance, mentorship, and community support
- 🌐 **Infrastructure Learning**: Azure-based labs and real-world system simulations
- 📚 **Consulting & Pathways**: Support for Umschulung, Weiterbildung, Ausbildung, and university access
- 🎥 **Content Creation**: Motivational media and tech literacy across platforms

---

## 🌍 Our Approach

We focus on:
- Inclusive, student-centered design
- Agile learning frameworks
- Real-world applications and problem-solving
- Community-first mindset

We’re not here to sell—we’re here to serve. Every repo, every resource, and every video is built to uplift, educate, and inspire.
